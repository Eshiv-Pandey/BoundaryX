# Limitations

The principal experiments are development evidence: authored deterministic tasks/receivers, actual SDK/bridge protocols, controlled faults and an upstream artifact fixture. They do not establish population loss prevalence, model ability, production authentication, every protocol surface, URI access, independent family sampling or naturally caused bridge faults.

The legacy runner/verifier share receiver and gold logic, establishing consistency/reconstruction rather than independent semantic correctness. The new SQLite oracle avoids those functions but shares task definitions, standard JSON/SQLite infrastructure and source/capture observations. Hashes and wire membership are not authenticated origin, causal completeness or independently observed receipt. Logical stdio packet recording excludes transport delimiters. Read-only fixture state is not a complete observer of external business effects. Separately implemented AI checks are not independent human scientific review.

Twenty structurally new held-out candidates are frozen with zero evaluations. Gold, reviewer acceptance and route support are Not Established. The existing receiver supports only the ten original families and cannot silently mock new families. Candidates are AI-assisted proposals, not independently designed human gold. Human changes require a new freeze. Final evaluation remains pending.

The 24-example human packet has blank judgments. Undergraduates assess clarity, consistency, output logic and traceability, not domain expertise. Supervisor review and external evaluation are pending.

Algo V1 refinement is a CEGAR/lazy-symbolic adaptation. The early comparator fairness defect and unfavorable corrected/shared-lifting/common-cost results remain explicit. Memoization, pruning, proof compression, contracts, fault injection and recovery are established ideas. General superiority, a new semantic theorem and scientific novelty are Not Established.

The original checkout, complete Git bundle, owned-source snapshot and original delivery archive remain local. Distribution redacts four older command-path records and changes only their provenance references/manifests, with a recorded digest chain. Scientific fields and all three principal v2 allocations are unchanged. Partial machine-path startup traces remain local. Some unchanged archived documents refer to original paths; active BoundaryX guides use current paths.

Fresh tsdown builds can change mgcrea hashed chunk names. Historical runners use licensed pinned build bytes restored by a hash-checking script plus separately installed frozen dependencies. This is a declared reproducibility constraint. Source-only wheels are not the complete artifact. No project-wide license, missing-license source rights, publication readiness, supervisor acceptance or completion percentage is invented.
