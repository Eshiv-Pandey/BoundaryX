## Candid novelty assessment

Established ideas: schema/contract checks, context propagation, task-bound effects, exact field/artifact comparison, controlled faults, exhaustive finite repair subsets, memoization, pruning and proof representation compression. Agent benchmarks and cross-protocol verification already exist.

Engineering contributions completed here: recoverable immutable evidence, separate source/sent/received accounting, task-relative required artifact fields, same-task authorized raw recovery, modular reproducibility, explicit unknown/coverage accounting and a separate SQL/wire verification path. These support an inspectable research artifact; they do not alone establish a novel scientific method.

Potentially distinctive empirical contribution: a paired task-relative preservation study across real independently implemented A2A/MCP routes that distinguishes artifact payload utility, metadata completeness, constraint/tool usability and recovery. The current fixture-specific findings can support this direction, but broader original tasks, human gold and untouched qualified evaluation are needed before calling the scope novel or generalizable.

Claims requiring further evidence: prevalence of heterogeneous-agent loss, LLM semantic transfer, comparative recovery benefit on held-out applications, new algorithmic/theoretical contribution, authenticated semantic correctness, production route conformance and advantage over existing boundary-repair frameworks. Status: Not Established. The project name and provisional alternative supply no novelty evidence.
